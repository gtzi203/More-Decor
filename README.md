## More Decor



This mod adds more decoration.



Features:

    -Cast iron ingot and node
    -Bricks in 7 different colors and in 5 different sizes
    -New glass types for normal- and obisdian glass + other new glass nodes
    -Industrial lamps in different sizes
    -Some signs in various forms and sizes
    -Metal sheets
    -New crafting mechanics with workbenches and press
    -Pans (there will be more furniture like this)

    I'm open to new ideas :)



Special Crafting:

    -Bricks:
        Medium bricks can be crafted like a normal brick. 
        The other forms can be crafted with the chisel table.

    Glass:
        The variations of normal- and obsidian glass can be crafted by using the glass workbench without any extra material.
        Other glass variants require an extra material, like steel for factory glass or cast iron for industrial glass.

    Signs:
        Use the signs workbench with an empty sign and dye.
